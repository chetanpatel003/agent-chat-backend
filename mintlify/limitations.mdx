---
title: Known limitations
description: What is not done, or not verified live.
---

- **No public deployment yet** — the runbook is ready; deployment needs the owner's Vercel login.
- **Merge Videos** accepts only your own uploaded videos (5 minutes combined at most); generated
  videos and external links are refused. Its admission estimate uses the measured upload durations
  and a 1.3× safety margin (decision 0018); the charge is always Magica's reported usage. No live
  merge has been run, so the pricing formula is documented but not confirmed against a real charge.
- **Authentication** uses Clerk session tokens; there are no long-lived API keys.
- **Live verification**: `generate_image` was verified against Magica; `edit_image`, `crop_image`
  and `merge_videos` are verified with recorded fixtures only.
- **Waiting runs** do not survive a Trigger.dev *dev* worker restart; the decision is then refused
  and the run ends as interrupted. Production checkpointing was not tested.
- **Scale**: load-tested to 200 concurrent admissions and 200 concurrent turns in one process with
  mocked providers (`project-docs/LOAD_TEST.md`); 1,000 concurrent turns was not measured.
- Webhook URL checks reject internal hostnames and literal private IPs, but not DNS names that
  resolve to private addresses.

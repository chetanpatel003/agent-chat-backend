---
title: Credits
description: Exactly-once settlement of media tool usage.
---

Model usage (OpenRouter free route) costs **zero** credits. Each Magica tool invocation is charged
the provider's own reported usage, in raw units (displayed ÷ 1,000,000 as "M").

- **Admission** before a billable call: fixed cost (crop) or a provider estimate (image
  generation, explicit size). Insufficient balance fails the tool call cleanly.
- **Settlement** as soon as the tool's outcome is recorded: one transaction locks the account row, claims the invocation
  once, debits and writes a ledger entry with a unique key — duplicates change nothing. The
  balance never goes negative; any shortfall is recorded.
- **Recovery**: a durable settlement task plus a 10-minute sweep settle anything missed.

`GET /api/v1/credits/balance` and `GET /api/v1/credits/ledger`.
